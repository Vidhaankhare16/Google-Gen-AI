RENTAL DEED / LEASE AGREEMENT

— — —

THIS DEED OF LEASE is executed at Bengaluru on this 1st day of April 2025 BETWEEN Mr. Rajesh Kumar, S/o Late Suresh Kumar, residing at 45 Residency Road, Bengaluru ("LESSOR") AND Ms. Ananya Sharma, D/o Mohan Sharma, presently residing at Pune ("LESSEE").

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of Flat No. 302, Sunrise Apartments, Indiranagar, Bengaluru ("the Premises") and has agreed to let out the same to the Lessee on the following terms.

1. TERM: The lease shall be for a period of 36 months commencing 1st April 2025. There is a lock-in period of the entire 36 months during which the Lessee cannot vacate.

2. RENT: The monthly rent shall be Rs. 55,000 payable in advance on or before the 5th of each month. The Lessor may revise the rent upward at his sole discretion at any time by giving 7 days notice, and the Lessee shall have no right to object to or contest such revision.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT: The Lessee shall pay an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 6,60,000 (equivalent to twelve months rent). This deposit is NON-REFUNDABLE and shall stand forfeited to the Lessor in all circumstances whatsoever, including normal expiry of the lease.

4. EARLY TERMINATION: Should the Lessee vacate before completion of the lock-in period for any reason whatsoever, the Lessee shall be liable to pay the entire balance rent for the unexpired period of 36 months immediately, as liquidated damages, in addition to forfeiture of the deposit.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS: The Lessee shall be solely responsible for all repairs to the Premises, including major structural repairs, waterproofing, and repairs to common areas, regardless of cause or fault.

6. TERMINATION BY LESSOR: The Lessor may terminate this lease at any time, with or without cause, without any notice, and re-enter and take possession of the Premises. The Lessee shall vacate immediately upon such termination.

7. SUB-LETTING: The Lessee shall not sub-let, assign, or part with possession of the Premises.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION: Any dispute arising out of this Deed shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator nominated solely by the Lessor, seated in Singapore, and governed by the laws of Singapore. The costs of arbitration shall be borne entirely by the Lessee.

9. INDEMNITY: The Lessee shall indemnify and keep indemnified the Lessor against any and all claims, losses, damages, and legal costs of any nature, without any monetary limit.

10. STAMP DUTY & REGISTRATION: The Lessee shall bear the stamp duty and registration charges. The parties agree that non-registration of this Deed shall not affect its enforceability.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have set their hands on the day and year first above written.

LESSOR: Rajesh Kumar LESSEE: Ananya Sharma
