PERSONAL LOAN AGREEMENT

This Loan Agreement is entered into on 10th June 2025 between QuickCash Finance Ltd. ("Lender")
and Mr. Vikram Reddy, residing at Nagpur ("Borrower").

1. LOAN AMOUNT: The Lender agrees to lend Rs. 5,00,000 to the Borrower ("the Loan").

2. INTEREST: The Loan shall carry interest at 36% per annum, compounded monthly. In the event of
   any default, the interest rate shall increase to 60% per annum on the entire outstanding amount.

3. PROCESSING & HIDDEN CHARGES: A processing fee of Rs. 50,000 shall be deducted upfront from the
   disbursed amount. The Lender may levy additional charges at its discretion without prior notice.

4. REPAYMENT: The Loan is repayable in 24 monthly instalments. Any delay of even one day in payment
   shall render the entire outstanding Loan immediately due and payable.

5. PREPAYMENT: The Borrower may not prepay the Loan. If prepayment is permitted by the Lender, a
   prepayment penalty of 5% of the outstanding principal shall apply.

6. SECURITY & PERSONAL GUARANTEE: The Borrower and his family members stand as personal guarantors
   and pledge their personal and immovable assets as security for the Loan without limit.

7. ACCELERATION & RECOVERY: On default, the Lender may take possession of the pledged assets
   without any court process and sell them, with any shortfall recoverable from the Borrower.

8. LATE FEES: A late payment fee of Rs. 5,000 per day of delay shall be charged, without any cap.

9. UNILATERAL VARIATION: The Lender may amend any term of this Agreement, including the interest
   rate and charges, unilaterally at any time, and such amendment shall bind the Borrower.

10. ARBITRATION & WAIVER: All disputes shall be settled by arbitration conducted by an arbitrator
    appointed by the Lender. The Borrower waives all rights to approach a consumer forum or court
    and waives the right to participate in any class action.

Lender: QuickCash Finance Ltd.               Borrower: Vikram Reddy
