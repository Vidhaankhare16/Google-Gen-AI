EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement is made on 15th May 2025 between TechNova Solutions Pvt. Ltd., a company
incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its office at Hyderabad ("Company") and
Mr. Arjun Mehta, residing at Chennai ("Employee").

1. POSITION: The Employee is appointed as Senior Software Engineer reporting to the CTO.

2. COMPENSATION: The Employee shall be paid a gross salary of Rs. 18,00,000 per annum, subject to
   statutory deductions. The Company may revise or reduce the compensation at its sole discretion.

3. PROBATION: The Employee shall be on probation for 12 months, extendable indefinitely at the
   Company's discretion, during which employment may be terminated without notice or reason.

4. NOTICE PERIOD: The Employee must serve a notice period of 6 months to resign. The Company may
   terminate the Employee's services at any time with 3 days notice or pay in lieu thereof.

5. NON-COMPETE: For a period of 3 years after leaving the Company, the Employee shall not, anywhere
   in India, be employed by, consult for, or be associated with any business that competes with the
   Company or operates in the information technology sector.

6. NON-SOLICITATION: For 2 years after separation, the Employee shall not solicit any client or
   employee of the Company.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY: All intellectual property created by the Employee during and for 12
   months after employment, whether or not related to the Company's business and whether created
   during working hours or personal time, shall belong exclusively to the Company.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY: The Employee shall keep all Company information confidential in perpetuity.

9. GARDEN LEAVE & CLAWBACK: Upon resignation the Company may place the Employee on garden leave
   without pay. Any joining bonus shall be clawed back in full if the Employee leaves within 3 years.

10. GOVERNING LAW: This Agreement is governed by Indian law. Any dispute shall be subject to the
    exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Hyderabad, and the Employee waives any objection to
    such jurisdiction or forum.

Signed for TechNova Solutions Pvt. Ltd.        Employee: Arjun Mehta
